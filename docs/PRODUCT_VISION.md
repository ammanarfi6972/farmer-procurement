# Product Vision

## Vision statement

Build a trustworthy, multilingual, real-time procurement coordination platform that helps farmers spend less time waiting, helps centres process arrivals more predictably, and gives administrators transparent operational visibility.

## Primary outcomes

### Farmer outcomes

- Know the correct centre.
- Know the appointment date/time.
- Know the booking/token.
- Know how many people are ahead.
- Know the estimated wait.
- Know what happened to the procurement.
- Know the payment status.
- Receive clear notifications in a preferred language.

### Centre outcomes

- Know who is expected.
- See who has arrived.
- Manage a live queue.
- Understand workload.
- Process farmers consistently.
- Reduce crowding and idle time.
- Keep an auditable record.

### Administration outcomes

- Compare centres.
- Detect overload.
- Understand demand and throughput.
- Monitor procurement and payment pipeline.
- Configure commodities, prices, schedules, capacities, and languages.

## Product differentiators

1. **Dynamic queue visibility** instead of a simple token.
2. **ETA based on live operational data**, not AI/ML.
3. **Capacity-aware slot scheduling**.
4. **Centre discovery with live availability and routing**.
5. **End-to-end procurement status timeline**.
6. **Configurable commodities and MSP/pricing**.
7. **Multilingual interface designed around farmer accessibility**.
8. **Role-specific operational dashboards**.
9. **Strong auditability and permissions**.

## Product personality

Trustworthy, calm, clear, modern, government-grade, human-centered.

Avoid:

- flashy consumer-app gimmicks;
- excessive animations;
- vague AI claims;
- overly technical language in farmer-facing screens;
- dark patterns;
- dense tables on mobile.
